Why You Might Need to Read a QR Code From a Photo

There are plenty of reasons you might have a QR code saved as a picture on your phone. A friend might text you a code for a restaurant menu. You might screenshot a code from a website because you want to open the link later. Maybe you saved a code from a social media post and forgot to scan it right away.

Whatever the reason, you now have a photo of a QR code sitting in your gallery. The problem is that your phone camera is designed to scan codes in the real world. It expects to see a code through the lens, not inside a photo album.

This means you need a slightly different approach to get the job done. The methods below will help you read the code directly from the image file. Each one takes less than a minute. You will get the link or information hidden inside the QR code without any extra hassle.

How to Scan a QR Code From a Screenshot on iPhone

Apple made this process very simple starting with iOS 15. If your iPhone runs iOS 15 or later, you already have a tool that can read QR codes from photos. The feature is called Live Text and it works automatically.

  1. Open the Photos app on your iPhone. Find the screenshot or saved image that contains the QR code. Tap on the image so it fills your entire screen.
  2. Look for the Live Text icon in the bottom right corner of the image. It looks like a tiny square with lines inside it. Tap it and your iPhone will highlight any text or codes it finds in the picture.
  3. Tap the detected link: If the QR code is clear and readable, a yellow notification banner will appear at the top of the screen. It will show the link or data stored inside the code. Tap that banner and your phone will open the link in Safari or the appropriate app.

If you do not see the Live Text icon, your iPhone might be running an older version of iOS. Go to Settings > General > Software Update to install the latest update.

Using the Files App: You can also save the QR code image to your Files folder. Open the file and long press on the QR code itself. A menu will pop up with an option to open the link. This method works well when the code is inside a PDF or a document you downloaded.

How to Scan a QR Code From a Screenshot on Android

Android phones give you a few different ways to read a QR code from a saved image. The easiest method uses Google Lens, which comes preinstalled on most Android devices.

  1. Open Google Photos or the Google app on your phone. Find the screenshot or photo that has the QR code in it. Tap on the image to open it full screen.
  2. Tap the Google Lens icon at the bottom of the screen. It looks like a small camera outline. Tap it and Google Lens will analyze the image.
  3. Open the link: Within a second or two, it will detect the QR code and display the link or information stored inside. Tap the result to open the link in your browser. The whole process takes about five seconds.

Samsung Galaxy Shortcut: Open the Gallery app and find your QR code image. Tap the three dots in the top right corner. Select the option that says Bixby Vision or Scan QR code. Your phone will read the code from the image and show you the result immediately.

Some Android phones also let you swipe down from the top of your screen to find the QR Scanner quick-settings tile. Tap it and choose the option to scan from a saved gallery photo.

How to Scan a QR Code From a Photo on Your Computer (PC & Mac)

Scanning a QR code from a saved image on your computer is just as easy as doing it on your phone. You do not need to install any new software or browser extensions.

Method 1: Right Click with Google Chrome

If you use Google Chrome, you have a fast built-in shortcut. Right click on any QR code image that appears on a webpage (or drag a saved screenshot into a Chrome tab). A menu will appear with an option that says "Search image with Google". Click it and Google Lens will open in a side panel on the right, reading the QR code and showing you the decoded link immediately.

Method 2: Use an Online QR Code Reader

Open your web browser and use a free online tool such as ScanQR or WebQR. Upload the screenshot or photo that contains the QR code, and the website will scan the image and display the hidden link or text within seconds.

Using Google Lens to Read a QR Code From Any Image

Google Lens is one of the most reliable cross-platform tools you can use to scan a QR code from a saved image or screenshot. On your phone, you can access Google Lens through the Google app or Google Photos. On your computer, you can use it through Chrome or by visiting lens.google.com.

Google Lens is smart enough to find QR codes even in busy images. If your screenshot has a lot of text and graphics around the code, Lens will still pick it out by focusing on the square pattern and ignoring everything else in the frame.

Pro Tip: Google Lens needs a clear image to work properly. If your screenshot is blurry or the QR code is tiny, zoom in or crop the screenshot before scanning so the code fills more of the frame.

Troubleshooting & Safety Tips When Scanning Saved QR Codes

Before opening any scanned link, keep these essential safety and troubleshooting tips in mind:

  • Always inspect the URL preview: Make sure the domain name matches the real service you intend to visit before clicking through.
  • Ensure high contrast: If the QR code won't scan, ensure all three square alignment markers in the corners are visible and not cropped out.
  • Never enter sensitive passwords or credentials on pages reached through an unsolicited QR code image.

Conclusion

Scanning a QR code from a saved picture or screenshot is simple once you know which tool to use. On iPhone, Live Text handles it directly in the Photos app. On Android and PC, Google Lens and free online scanners like ScanQR provide instant results without hassle.
